SPATIAL INPUT SYSTEM / PROTOTYPE 01

Control the interface.
Without touching it.

AURA turns your front camera into a local hand-tracking input. Start the camera, hold one hand in view and test the gesture controls below.

LOCAL CAMERA PROCESSINGCamera permission is requested only when you start AURA. The camera feed stays hidden. Frames are processed on this device; this page does not upload or record them. The vision library and model are downloaded from their providers, and MediaPipe may send SDK usage/performance metrics.

AURA control

Camera activation is manual. Use Stop AURA to release the camera immediately. AURA also stops the camera when this page is hidden or closed.

OFF — camera not active
WAITING FOR HAND

Gesture legend

Keep your hand approximately 30–70 cm from the camera. Good lighting improves tracking. Move slowly until the cursor stabilizes.

INDEX — MOVE
PINCH — CLICK
TWO FINGERS — SCROLL
OPEN PALM — CANCEL / RESUME
FIST — EMERGENCY PAUSE

Interaction field

Use your index finger to move the cursor, then pinch over the action button to activate it. The escape control appears only while AURA is active.

Activated: 0 times

How to test

01 / STARTPress Start AURA and grant camera permission.
02 / MOVERaise one hand and point with your index finger.
03 / CLICKTouch thumb and index finger together over the test button.
04 / PAUSEClose your hand into a fist. Open your palm to resume.
05 / STOPPress Stop AURA. The camera indicator should turn off.
Scroll gesture area

Extend your index and middle fingers. Move both fingers upward slowly to scroll the page. Keep the other fingers folded.

Two-finger scroll is page-level in this prototype. It will be mapped to spatial panels when AURA is integrated into the complete website.

Camera frames are not uploaded by this page. All frame inference is local to the browser.
