Who is Khayom Abdurahmonov?

Khayom Abdurahmonov is a dentistry student and the founder of VEYROST, an independent technology company and long-term innovation project originating from Tajikistan.

His work focuses on exploring artificial intelligence, software development, automation, personal computing and experimental technology.

What is VEYROST?

VEYROST is not limited to a single product or technology. Its long-term direction is to develop technologies, products, services and projects across multiple fields.
